WhiteArc Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-27

WhiteArc is a meal planner that keeps your data on your device. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your device

Foods, meals, plans, goals, body weight, supplements, training program, working weights and day photos are stored as files in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them unless you exported a copy first (Settings, Export vault).

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy the app's files (photos excluded) to your Google account so they can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Google under your Google account settings and is encrypted by Android. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to fetch the product's name and nutrition. Nothing else is sent: no account, no location, no other food data. Open Food Facts receives your IP address as part of any web request; see their privacy policy. Product data comes from Open Food Facts under the Open Database License.

Camera

The camera is used only while you scan a barcode or take a day photo. Barcode detection runs on your device using Google's ML Kit code scanner, which is provided by Google Play services. Google may collect diagnostic and performance information about that component, as described in Google's ML Kit terms. Images are not sent anywhere by WhiteArc.

Optional support purchases

Support tiers are one-time Google Play purchases that unlock nothing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

The optional supplement reminder is scheduled on your phone. No push service is used.

Sharing and selling

We do not collect, share or sell personal data.

Children

WhiteArc is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
