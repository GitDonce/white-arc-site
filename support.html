Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in WhiteArc's own app storage. There is no account and no server, so nobody else has a copy.

How do I keep a copy?

Settings, Your data, Export vault. It makes a zip of every file (plain text Markdown plus your photos) and opens the share sheet so you can save it to Drive, email it to yourself, or keep it anywhere else.

I got a new phone. Will my data come with me?

If Google backup is on (Android Settings, Google, Backup), Android restores WhiteArc's data when you install it on the new phone with the same Google account. Day photos are not part of that backup. Export the vault before you switch phones anyway, just in case.

Does uninstalling delete everything?

Yes. Uninstalling removes all WhiteArc data from the phone. Export first if you want to keep it.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings, Apps, WhiteArc, Storage, Clear storage. To remove the Google backup copy too, delete it under your Google account's backups in Google Drive.

Barcodes

A barcode was not found

Lookups go to Open Food Facts first, then USDA FoodData Central. If neither has the product, create the food by hand from the label. It is saved for next time. You can also add the product to Open Food Facts so it works for everyone.

The numbers on a scanned food look wrong

Product data comes from public databases and is sometimes off. Edit the food and fix it; your version is the one WhiteArc uses from then on.

The scanner will not open

The first scan downloads a small Google scanner module, so it needs internet once. If camera access was denied, allow it under Android Settings, Apps, WhiteArc, Permissions.

Everything else

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no locked features. The tips in Settings are optional and unlock nothing. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Can I use kJ or pounds?

Yes. Settings, Display.

Is WhiteArc medical or dietary advice?

No. It is a planning and tracking tool. Talk to a professional for medical or dietary decisions.
